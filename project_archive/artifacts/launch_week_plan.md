## 3. Stockout Prevention: Historical Failures vs. New Safeguards

| Historical Failure Mode (October AlloyDB Crisis) | Root Cause in Ledger / Reviews | Mandatory Store #7 Launch Safeguard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. The "Front-Load and Freeze" Trap** | Large bulk drop on Oct 5 followed by **3 consecutive weeks of zero deliveries**, causing complete stockouts of matcha and beans. | **Bi-Weekly Split Deliveries**: Mandatory delivery split of **60% on Monday 5:30 AM** and **40% on Thursday 5:30 AM**. No cancellation of replenishment runs regardless of current stock. |
| **2. Multi-SKU Raw Ingredient Bottleneck (Oat Milk)** | Oat milk shortage paralyzed 3 top-selling drinks simultaneously, triggering waves of 2-star reviews across Yelp and DoorDash. | **Dedicated Ingredient Par**: Raw oat milk buffered at **60 cases on-site** (separate from dairy milk), with an automated trigger for emergency reorder if on-hand volume drops below 20 cases. |
| **3. High-Margin Retail Bean Depletion** | SoMa (`STR-001`) dwindled to 2 bags of Guatemala Geisha (`SKU-502`), disappointing repeat customers who drove across town. | **Dedicated Retail Safety Stock**: Par level set at **45 bags** for Geisha and **50 bags** for Ethiopia Yirgacheffe; restocked every Thursday. |
| **4. Lagging Cold Brew Prep Deficit** | Hayes Valley ran out of concentrate, serving watery coffee. | **Continuous Cold Brew Tapping**: Minimum of **100 gallons of concentrate pre-steeped on a rolling 48-hour schedule**, maintaining at least two active kegs in reserve. |
| **5. Unrealistic AlloyDB Reorder Thresholds** | Reorder thresholds (e.g. 45 units in Mission) were dwarfed by rapid daily sales burn (464 units/wk). | **Dynamic Reorder Trigger**: Reorder threshold calibrated to **48 hours of peak sales velocity** rather than static store minimums. |
